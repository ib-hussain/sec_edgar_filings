## SEC Filings
Use SEC Fillings entire pipeline for training the quantitative analyst and also the fusion module but remove that whole branch for inference purposes and for specialisation testing cases

**Financial PhraseBank (the 4,845/4,840-sentence set):**
- Source: a financial corpus called TRC2-financial, a subset of Reuters' TRC2 with 1.8M news articles published between 2008-2010 was used for pretraining; the actual sentiment-labeled set is Financial PhraseBank from Malo et al. 2014, consisting of 4845 English sentences selected randomly from financial news found on LexisNexis, annotated by 16 people with finance/business backgrounds
- Download: `huggingface.co/datasets/takala/financial_phrasebank` (also `gtfintechlab/financial_phrasebank_sentences_allagree`)
- Four agreement-level configs available: sentences_50agree, 66agree, 75agree, allagree (100% agreement = 2264 sentences) — higher agreement = cleaner but smaller
- License note: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 — non-commercial only, relevant if this ever leaves academic use

**SEC 8-K + price reaction (event-study labeled):** 
- a standardized abnormal reaction measure divides the return by the stock's normal daily volatility over a 60-day baseline window — this is the kind of self-labeling approach usable for your own dataset construction (arXiv 2607.08346, "Grounded Event Extraction from SEC 8-K Filings")

**Form 4 insider trading:** 
- raw structured data via SEC EDGAR; one study built unlawful/lawful labels by matching Form 4 filer names against SEC litigation complaints using Levenshtein distance matching, yielding 1,992 labeled unlawful transactions — a rare case of a genuinely *labeled* insider dataset (arXiv 2411.13564)

**Self-labeling at scale (FinGPT approach — worth copying):** 
- FinGPT adopts the percentage of relative stock price change following a news article as the output label itself, thresholding the continuous return into positive/negative/neutral — a self-labeled approach that aligns sentiment with actual market reaction and avoids manual annotation cost. This is probably your most scalable path forward for the filings/insider-news problem.

**StockTwits:** 
- cashtag-based microblogging, has explicit bullish/bearish tagging by users, heavily used in academic sentiment literature. One study built a vocabulary of one million investor sentiment-labeled terms from StockTwits messages posted between January 2010 and March 2020
